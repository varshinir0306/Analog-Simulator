# Energy Band Diagram Simulator

This project is an interactive Python-based simulator for visualizing the **energy band diagrams of PN Junction, BJT, and MOSFET**.

The application shows how the **conduction band, valence band, and Fermi level** vary when different voltages are applied to semiconductor devices.

### Features

* PN Junction energy band diagram
* BJT energy band diagram
* MOSFET energy band diagram
* Adjustable applied voltage using sliders
* Visualization of band bending
* Semiconductor equations with explanations
* Simple and interactive Streamlit interface

### Technologies Used

* Python
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Streamlit

### Objective

The main objective is to understand and visualize the relationship between **applied voltage and energy-band variation** in semiconductor devices.

### Devices Covered

1. **PN Junction** – Shows variation of the potential barrier under forward and reverse bias.
2. **BJT** – Shows the effect of base-emitter voltage on the emitter-base energy barrier.
3. **MOSFET** – Shows surface band bending with variation in gate-source voltage.

### Note

The diagrams are **conceptual/illustrative models** created for educational purposes to demonstrate the effect of applied voltage on semiconductor energy bands.
